## Implementation tracking

The checkboxes above mean **identified in the official sitemap** only. They do not mean implemented, formula-reviewed, tested, or verified.

Recommended per-calculator status fields for the project registry:

- `discovered`
- `requirements_researched`
- `formula_documented`
- `implemented`
- `unit_tested`
- `ui_verified`
- `seo_complete`

A calculator should be marked complete only after its inputs, outputs, assumptions, validation, edge cases, formula explanation, suitable visualization (if useful), and automated tests have been reviewed.

## Research still required before implementation

The sitemap establishes the official calculator names and categories, but does not provide enough detail to safely specify every calculator's complete input schema, formula, assumptions, outputs, or edge cases. Those must be researched from the individual calculator pages and, where appropriate, reliable domain references before implementation.

Pay special attention to country-specific tools (for example, Income Tax, UK Mortgage, Canadian Mortgage, FHA and VA Mortgage, and 401K), health-related limitations and disclaimers, and calculators whose names overlap but whose calculation models differ.
